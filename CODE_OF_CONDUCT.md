# Code of Conduct

AI Alchemist is a small open-source developer tool. People who use it, read it, or send patches are expected to treat one another with ordinary professional respect.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good faith in review comments.
- Critique the change, not the person.
- Keep discussion on the code, the documentation, and the security boundaries.
- Accept a "no" on patches that would execute prompts as shell commands, hide a remote provider behind a local label, or phone home with experiment history.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, slurs, or sexual attention aimed at another person.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a change that the maintainers have declined because it breaks the read-only or local-only rules.

## Reporting

Open an issue in this repository when it has a public issue tracker, or contact the maintainers through the forge that hosts the code. This document does not list a private email address, because the project does not publish one.

Maintainers may reject a patch or a conversation that breaks these expectations. There is no separate enforcement committee.

This is the project's own short standard. It is not a claim that AI Alchemist has adopted a particular foundation's enforcement process.
